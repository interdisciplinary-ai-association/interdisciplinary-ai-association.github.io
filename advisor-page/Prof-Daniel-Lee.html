Professor Daniel Hong-Seng LEE

Associate Vice-President, Office of Innovation and Enterprise @ CUHK

Honorary Professor, School of Biomedical Sciences, The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Adjunct Professor, Division of Life Science, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology

BSc (Hons) (HK); PhD, Pathology (HK)

Biography

Professor Daniel HS Lee is an accomplished leader in biomedical sciences and innovation. As Associate Vice-President (Innovation and Enterprise) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) and Honorary Professor at the School of Biomedical Sciences, he bridges academia and industry.


As a clinical biochemist by training, Professor Lee has extensive experience in the pharmaceutical industry locally and globally. He served in pharma companies, including Johnson and Johnson USA, Biogen USA and GSK China, for over 25 years. He has served as the CEO, board director and consultant for various biotech companies, including being the General Manager and Head of site for Roche Pharma R&D China. His expertise encompasses a wide spectrum of drug discovery and development, ranging from small molecule and biologic compounds to advanced therapeutic interventions, including companion diagnostics and device instrumentation across various therapeutic domains like central nervous system disorders, infectious diseases, diabetes, ophthalmology, oncology, and inflammation.


Professor Lee has been instrumental in driving the growth of the biotech and healthcare sectors in Hong Kong, leveraging his leadership to promote advanced therapeutics, medical devices, translational medicine, automation, and big data applications within the region. This commitment is further demonstrated by his previous role as Head of the Biomedical Technology Cluster at the Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks Corporation (2013-2018), where he significantly contributed to establishing the biotech/healthcare industry as a key economic driver for Hong Kong.


Professor Lee's scholarly achievements are exemplified by his publication of over 50 peer-reviewed papers, active involvement in teaching, acquisition of international research grants, contribution to journal manuscript reviews, and co-invention of 16 published patents.


Professor Lee is dedicated to enhancing the quality of local and global industry and research community. He has been an Advisory Committee Member of the School of Pharmacy at CUHK, Scientific Advisor of the Clinical Trial Centre at the Prince of Wales Hospital, International Human Epigenetics Ethics Committee member, Logistic and Supply Chain Multitech Board member, and the Innovation and Technology Commission ITF Research Project Assessment Panel. He also actively empowers students’ development, such as mentoring at the Hong Kong Life Science Society and supports various student-led initiatives, encouraging an inclusive and dynamic learning environment.
