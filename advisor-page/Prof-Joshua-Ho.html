Biography

Professor Joshua Ho is the Assistant Dean (Innovation & Technology Transfer) at the Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine of the University of Hong Kong (HKUMed). He holds a BSc (Hon 1, Medal) and a PhD in Bioinformatics from the University of Sydney, with postdoctoral training at Harvard Medical School. He is an expert in bioinformatics and digital health, with research expertise in single cell genomics, metagenomics, AI in medicine, and digital health technology. Professor Ho is also the Deputy Director of EdTech (AI) and Lead Scientist of Laboratory Data Discovery for Health at HKUMed. He founded a digital health company Vitome Limited.
