Biography

Dr. Jack Tsao serves as the Associate Director and Senior Lecturer of the Common Core, an interdisciplinary undergraduate curriculum at the University of Hong Kong (HKU). He earned his Master of Applied Finance and PhD in Education from the University of Queensland, with a focus on comparative and international education, youth aspirations, and the sociology of education. Dr. Tsao is a Senior Fellow of Higher Education Advance, bringing extensive experience in social impact, experiential learning, and project-based initiatives. His passion for educational innovation is evident in his emphasis on undergraduate research, community partnerships, and the integration of digital technologies (especially artificial intelligence), gaming, and storytelling in education. He has authored numerous articles for leading educational journals and has been a featured speaker at international conferences. In 2022, Dr. Tsao was part of the team that received the HKU Outstanding Teaching Awards for the Communication-Intensive Courses Initiative and played a pivotal role in winning the International QS Silver Awards for the Common Core.


Dr. Tsao held several leadership positions in organizational learning and development, commercial banking, and entrepreneurship. He was an associate consultant at RogenSi (TeleTech), a global performance and leadership consulting firm, where he worked with senior executives and professionals from Fortune 500 companies to enhance client capabilities and boost team productivity and effectiveness. Additionally, he successfully managed a startup distribution company as the CEO, growing its annual revenue to USD 40 million and extending its reach across 13 markets in the Asia Pacific region.
