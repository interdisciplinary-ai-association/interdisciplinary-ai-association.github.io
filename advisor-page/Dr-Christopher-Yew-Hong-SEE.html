Biography

Dr. Christopher See is a distinguished medical educator with a rich academic background, having obtained his medical degree from the University of Cambridge (Trinity College), a PGCE from the University of Edinburgh, and a Ph.D. in Medical Education from The University of Hong Kong (HKU). Currently serving as a lecturer in anatomy at the School of Biomedical Sciences, Dr. See has been recognized for his exceptional teaching methodologies, earning teaching excellence awards from both the University of Manchester and CUHK.


With his research focusing on Artificial Intelligence (AI) and gamification within medical education, Dr. See has secured grants exceeding 5.6 million HKD, leading to numerous publications, keynote speeches at conferences, and invited lectures in this field. He has taught AI and Machine Learning modules for healthcare across various medical schools. He leads both the AI for Education Community of Practice in CUHK and a Hong Kong-wide 1.6 million HKD TDLEG initiative “AI for Education” with 5 partner institutions.


Recognized for his outstanding contributions to higher education, he has been honoured with Fellowships from the Academy of Medical Educators UK (FAcadMed) and the Higher Education Academy (FHEA). In 2022, his excellence in teaching was further acknowledged with the Faculty Education Award.


Central to Dr. See's work is the establishment of a 'virtual lab' for educational research and innovation, where he empowers medical students to actively participate in the research and development process. This collaborative approach has yielded remarkable outcomes, with student lab members producing first-author journal papers, delivering conference presentations, and receiving research awards in medical education.


Through the development of advanced AI educational tools and a crowdsourcing teaching framework to produce e-learning resources and scholarly works, Dr. See and his team have made significant contributions to enhancing learning experiences. Their initiatives, including AI conversation agents and new interdisciplinary teaching materials, have received prestigious awards such as the Gold Award for Pedagogical Innovation at CUHK and the Exemplary Teaching and Learning award by eLFA in 2021.


Emphasizing student-teacher partnerships, Dr. See has demonstrated the transformative impact of collaborative educational research. His commitment to establishing engaging learning environments and his dedication to continuous improvement underscores his passion for advancing medical education. As a beacon of inspiration within academia, Dr. See embodies a spirit of innovation, collaboration, and excellence in medical education, paving the way for a future where student empowerment, scholarly advancement, and technological innovation thrive hand in hand.
