Biography

Professor Gary KK Lau is a distinguished figure in the medical and academic community, with significant contributions to healthcare, education, and technology innovation. In recognition of his exceptional leadership and dedication, he was recently elected as the ‘10 Outstanding Young Persons’ of 2024.


As the Assistant Dean (Education Innovations) and the Director of Educational Technology & Student in Medical and Health Sciences Education (SIMHSE) at the Li Ka Shing Faculty of Medicine, The University of Hong Kong, Professor Lau leads various innovative initiatives to enhance the medical curriculum and empower students’ professional development, including the integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in medical education and the promotion of educator-student partnership. He is also the Founding Editor of the HKU eLearning Platform in Clinical Neurosciences.


Clinically, Professor Lau is dedicated to advancing stroke patient care, especially through technological innovations. He spearheads the Stroke Research and Prevention Group (HKU Stroke) and pioneered the development of the app WeRISE, a one-stop platform supporting stroke patients and their families. He is also the co-founder of Remobility and leads the development of SmartRehab, an AI mobile platform that helps facilitate stroke rehabilitation in the home setting.


Professor Lau’s academic impacts have been recognised globally. He authored more than 170 research articles and book chapters about stroke, which have been featured in renowned publications such as Lancet Neurology, Lancet Infectious Diseases, European Heart Journal, Nature Communications, JAMA Neurology, Neurology, and Stroke.


As a prominent regional leader in stroke, Professor Lau serves as a Director of the Hong Kong Stroke Fund, Honorary Secretary of the Hong Kong Stroke Society, and an Honorary Advisor for the Hong Kong Stroke Association and Hong Kong Society for Rehabilitation. Professor Lau’s contributions also extend internationally. He is a Founding Steering Committee Member of the Microbleeds International Collaborative Network (MICON), Steering Committee Member and Country Lead of the International Multicentre Patent Foramen Ovale and Stroke registry (IMPROVE) and Founding Committee Member of the Neurological and Mental Health Global Epidemiology Network (NeuroGEN).


In recognition of his clinical, research and teaching excellence, Professor Lau has been awarded the Paul Dudley White International Scholar Award, American Heart Association Award for Patient-Oriented or Clinical Research in Hypertension, Hong Kong West Cluster Outstanding Team Award for Comprehensive Acute Stroke Service; Faculty Teaching Medal, British Medical Association Medical Book Award and Smart Ageing Award (Gold Award).
